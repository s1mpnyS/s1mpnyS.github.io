_Academic project, Feb – Jul 2025._

A discrete-event simulation model built in **Arena** to evaluate how automation technologies could be integrated at Tan Cang – Cai Mep Container Terminal. The model follows containers from vessel arrival through quay cranes, horizontal transport and the yard to the gate, and was used to:

- identify operational bottlenecks across terminal resources,
- test resource allocation scenarios to raise system throughput,
- assess the efficiency of a smart port configuration.
