I am a B.Eng. student in Logistics and Supply Chain Management at International University, VNU-HCM, working in the Human–Machine Interface Laboratory. I want to build a research career in **operations research and artificial intelligence**, developing optimization methods and decision-making algorithms for real logistics and supply chain problems.

My current work sits between combinatorial optimization and deep reinforcement learning:

- **Neural combinatorial optimization.** A Lookahead-Aware Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning (LA-HRL) framework for the Fleet Size and Mix Vehicle Routing Problem, with attention-based encoder–decoders (POMO-style, GAT) trained by PPO.
- **Learning-guided metaheuristics.** Adaptive Large Neighborhood Search (ALNS) warm-started or guided by learned policies, applied to heterogeneous-fleet routing and to parallel batch processing machine scheduling with incompatible families.
- **Time-dependent routing.** Coordinated repair of joint replenishment decisions in time-dependent inventory routing under FIFO travel times.

Outside research, I am a teaching assistant in the lab, served as Vice Head of the IEM Academic Club, and sat on the academic committee of the Vietnam Young Logistics Talents Competition.
